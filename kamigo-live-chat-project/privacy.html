1. Who we are

Kamigo ("we", "us") connects customers with local home-service providers. Business details: [Business legal name], [Address, Mangalagiri, Andhra Pradesh, India]. Contact for privacy questions: [privacy@your-domain.com].

2. What we collect

  • Account: name, email address and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Profile: phone number, age, gender, city, home and work address that you enter.
  • Bookings: service, date and time, service address, PIN code, coupon code, optional photos you upload, and the status of your request.
  • Support chat and reviews: messages you send us, and ratings or comments you leave.
  • Technical data: basic error reports (browser type, page, error message) when something fails while you are signed in.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account and let you book, change or cancel services.
  • To contact you about your request (phone, email, chat).
  • To share the details needed for a job with the technician assigned to it.
  • To show service ratings, prevent misuse, fix errors and improve the service.

Our legal basis is your consent (given when you save your profile) and the need to perform the service you request. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

4. Who can see your data

  • Kamigo staff (administrators and support) can see your profile, bookings and chats.
  • Assigned technicians see only the job details for bookings assigned to them (name, phone, address, schedule, photos).
  • Other customers never see your data. Reviews show only your first name, rating and comment.
  • Service providers we use: Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, hosting, email delivery) processes data on our behalf. Data may be stored on servers outside India.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Profile and chat data are kept while your account is active. Booking records may be kept for up to [3 years] for accounting, disputes and legal requirements unless you ask for erasure (see below), after which they are deleted unless the law requires us to retain them.

6. Your rights

  • Access and correct your details any time under My profile.
  • Delete your account and data: use My profile → Delete my account & data. Your profile, bookings, chats, reviews and photos are erased, normally within 7 days.
  • Withdraw consent, ask questions or make a complaint by contacting us at the address above.

7. Children

Kamigo is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by access rules so customers can read only their own data. No online service is perfectly secure; please keep your Google account secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Significant changes will be highlighted on the website.
